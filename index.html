FinGuard

Azure Databricks PySpark Confluent Kafka Delta Lake Medallion Flow
Architecture Overview

FinGuard is an end-to-end streaming data pipeline engineered to ingest, clean, score, and detect credit card fraud in real-time. Built around a Delta Lake medallion architecture with zero data loss.

1. Bronze Layer: Raw Ingestion
Kafka streaming topics → PySpark Structured Streaming → Append-only Delta Tables.

2. Silver Layer: Cleaned & Validated
Schema enforcement, deduplication, timestamp normalization, and feature transformations.

3. Gold Layer: Business & Fraud Signals
Real-time risk scoring, anomaly detection, automated email alert triggers, and BI aggregates.
Key Engineering Highlights

• Low-Latency Processing: Micro-batch streaming with sub-second ingestion latency across distributed PySpark clusters.
• ACID Guarantees: Delta Lake transaction logs prevent partial commits and enable time-travel auditability.
• Automated Alerting: Triggered instant notification workflows on high-risk transaction anomalies detected at the Gold stage.
